Chess, but one of your pawns is a traitor

Rules

Recruit a sleeper. Mature it. Defect it.

Standard chess plus one hidden allegiance: before the game, you secretly turn one of your opponent's pawns into your agent. Keep it alive and it can defect into a Knight, Rook — even a Queen. Suspect a traitor in your own ranks? Interrogate.

A defecting sleeper's power scales with how long it survives and how deep it sits — so an early reveal is just a pawn, and the enemy arms your agent every time they push it. Full rules →
